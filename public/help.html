Plans & billing (businesses)

What do the plans cost?
StarterNZ$29 + GST a monthStamp cards and rewards, reward alerts, customer list, fixed staff PIN
StandardNZ$49 + GST a monthEverything in Starter, plus Featured at the top of Discover in your suburb and a rotating staff PIN

New businesses start with a free trial. You're never charged during it.

How do I choose, change or cancel my plan?

Log in to your dashboard at stampd.co.nz → Vendor Login, then open Plan & Billing.

On a free trial: choose the plan you'd like after your trial — nothing is charged until the trial ends. Once you have a plan, Manage billing lets you switch plan, update your card, see invoices or cancel.

Prefer us to do it? Email support@stampd.co.nz with your business name.

What happens when my free trial ends?

We email you 30 days and 7 days before it ends. If you haven't chosen a plan by then, stamping keeps working for 14 more days, then pauses until you choose one. Nothing is ever deleted — your customers' cards and stamps are kept safe, and everything switches back on the moment you pick a plan.

Will my customers lose their stamps if I change plan?

No. Every card and reward stays exactly as it is, whatever plan you're on.

How do I get an invoice?

Dashboard → Plan & Billing lists your payments with a PDF for each. Prices are GST-exclusive; your invoices show the GST.

Running Stampd at your counter

How do customers collect a stamp?

They open the Stampd app, tap Scan and scan your counter QR code. If you require a PIN, they hand you their phone and you type your staff PIN.

Print your QR code from the dashboard: View QR Code → Print for the counter.

The customer's camera won't scan

In the app they tap Enter PIN with Vendor and type your 8-character business code (printed under your QR code). Then you type your staff PIN.

A regular came back later and couldn't get a stamp

You choose how often a customer can collect a stamp: every 2, 4 or 6 hours, once a day (the default), or every 2 or 3 days. Change it in Settings, in the app or on the dashboard. If someone tries too soon, they're told kindly when their next stamp is ready.

A customer earned a reward — what now?

The customer taps the reward in their app and scans your counter QR code. A big green "Claimed just now" screen appears with the time ticking — that's your cue to hand it over. It's used up there and then, so it can't be shown twice. If their camera won't scan, you can type your staff PIN instead.

What about when there's no internet?

Customers who've been to you before can still collect a stamp: you type your staff PIN and the stamp is saved on their phone, then added when they're back online. Rewards can be claimed by scanning, too. A customer's very first stamp with you needs internet.

Where is my staff PIN? Can I choose my own?

In the app: menu → Staff PIN. On the dashboard: Settings → Staff PIN. You can generate a new one or choose your own — easy ones like 1234 or 0000 aren't allowed. Keep it to staff only.

Can I change my reward after customers have started?

Yes. Customers part-way through a card keep their deal and are offered the choice to switch. Nobody loses stamps.

Customers

I got a new phone — are my stamps gone?

No. Your cards live in your Stampd account, not on your phone. Install Stampd, sign in with the same email, and everything is there.

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot Password? on the sign-in screen. The reset email comes from Stampd — check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

Still stuck? Email support@stampd.co.nz — tell us your business name or the email on your account and what happened.
